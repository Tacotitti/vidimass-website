About MediaMass

We built the infrastructure that turns unknown tracks into chart hits. Here's how it happened — and why it works.

Our Story

MediaMass started from a simple observation: the music industry was spending millions on traditional PR and radio promotion while a single viral TikTok video could do more for a track's chart position in 48 hours than months of conventional marketing.

We're a team of engineers, data scientists, and music industry veterans who set out to build the most reliable mass-distribution infrastructure on social media. What began as an experiment with a few thousand accounts quickly grew into a network of over 250,000 active TikTok profiles — all maintained, managed, and deployed by our proprietary automation systems.

Today, MediaMass operates at scale that no individual account management team could match. We've helped hundreds of artists — from bedroom producers to platinum-selling acts — achieve genuine chart placements and break through the noise of an overwhelmingly competitive streaming landscape.

We're not a bot farm. We're not a vanity metrics machine. We're a precision reach tool, and we stand behind every campaign we run with a results guarantee.

What We Do

TikTok Mass Posting

We distribute your video content to up to 250,000 TikTok accounts simultaneously. Each post goes live with unique captions, targeted hashtags, and natural timing to maximize organic reach and engagement. This is the fastest way to generate the stream count and virality signals that move the needle on charts.

Instagram Mass Reels

Our Instagram network extends your content's reach across thousands of Reels-active accounts. Instagram's algorithm rewards content that gains traction quickly — our distribution gives your video the momentum it needs to enter the Explore feed organically.

Spotify Charting Campaigns

We've turned mass posting into a precise Spotify charting tool. By engineering the right engagement signals at the right scale, we push tracks into algorithmic playlists and viral charts. Our 100% success rate on Spotify charting campaigns is not an accident — it's the result of refined methodology built over thousands of campaigns.

Social Media Charting

Beyond Spotify, we help artists dominate TikTok sound charts, Apple Music viral charts, and platform-specific trending lists. Our social media charting service is a comprehensive music promotion package designed for maximum chart impact.

Our Approach to Safety

Mass posting at scale is only sustainable if it's done right. We've seen many short-term operations in this space burn out because they prioritized volume over quality. We built MediaMass differently.

  • ✓ Human-like behavior: Our accounts post with realistic timing variation, natural engagement, and content history that mirrors real users.
  • ✓ Content-first distribution: We review all campaign content to ensure it meets platform quality standards before deployment.
  • ✓ No client account involvement: We never require access to your personal or brand social media accounts. Campaigns run entirely through our network.
  • ✓ Continuous network maintenance: We constantly refresh and add new accounts to keep network health high and campaign performance consistent.
