Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about mass posting, Spotify charting, and running campaigns with MediaMass.

General Questions

What is mass posting? ▼

Mass posting is the automated simultaneous distribution of video content to thousands of social media accounts. Instead of publishing one video to one profile, our system deploys your content across up to 250,000 TikTok and Instagram accounts at once. The effect is exponential reach — what would take a single account months to achieve, mass posting can accomplish in days. It's the core technology behind viral music moments, trending challenges, and rapid chart climbs.

Who uses mass posting services? ▼

Our clients range from independent artists pushing their debut singles to major record labels running coordinated release campaigns. Brands use mass posting to launch viral marketing initiatives. Music managers use it to give their artists a competitive edge. If your goal is maximum reach on TikTok or Instagram in the shortest possible time, mass posting is the most direct path there.

How many accounts are in your network? ▼

Our active network includes 250,000+ TikTok accounts and thousands of Instagram accounts. Each account is maintained with realistic posting history, profile completeness, and natural engagement behavior. We continuously add and refresh accounts to maintain network health and campaign effectiveness.

What countries can you target? ▼

We offer geo-targeted campaigns for the US, UK, Germany, France, Netherlands, Australia, Canada, and other major markets. Global campaigns (no region restriction) typically generate the highest raw stream numbers. Regional targeting is ideal when you need to qualify for country-specific charts or reach a defined audience. Contact us to discuss targeting requirements for your campaign.

Spotify Charting

How does mass posting help with Spotify charting? ▼

Spotify's algorithm heavily weighs engagement signals from TikTok and Instagram. When your music-linked video gets deployed to 250,000 accounts, thousands of real users discover the track, click through to Spotify, and stream it. The resulting spike in streams, saves, and playlist adds pushes your track into algorithmic playlists like Discover Weekly and Release Radar. From there, organic momentum takes over. We've achieved chart placement for 100% of our Spotify charting campaigns.

How long until I see chart results? ▼

Content distribution happens within 24–72 hours. Chart movement typically appears within 7–14 days, though some tracks chart within 3–5 days when engagement velocity is high. Factors like track quality, existing audience, and release timing all influence speed.

Which Spotify charts can you target? ▼

Our campaigns are most effective for Spotify's algorithmic playlists (Discover Weekly, Release Radar, Daily Mix), viral charts (Spotify Viral 50), and regional charts in Germany, Austria, Switzerland, UK, and the US. We can discuss specific chart targets during campaign planning.

Technical & Safety

What video formats do you accept? ▼

We accept MP4 (H.264 codec) video files. For TikTok and Instagram Reels, a 9:16 vertical aspect ratio performs best. Recommended resolution is 1080×1920. Maximum file size is 2 GB. Videos should be between 15 seconds and 3 minutes for optimal reach.

Is mass posting safe for my account or brand? ▼

Yes. We post from our own network of accounts — your personal TikTok or Instagram profile is never involved unless you specifically request it. Our network accounts operate with natural posting schedules, varied captions, and human-like behavior patterns. We've run thousands of campaigns with a 99.9% success rate and no brand safety incidents.

How do I submit my content? ▼

After booking your campaign via our contact form, we send you a secure upload link. You upload your video file and optional caption/hashtag preferences. Our team reviews the submission and begins deployment within one business day.
