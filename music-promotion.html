The Problem With Music Promotion in 2026

The music industry releases over 100,000 tracks every single day. Traditional PR campaigns, playlist pitching, and even paid ads struggle to break through that level of noise. An artist can spend €5,000 on promotion and see zero chart movement because the effort is spread too thin across too many channels.

Meanwhile, tracks with no promotion budget go viral overnight — not by accident, but because someone engineered the right conditions. A single TikTok moment can drive 100,000 Spotify streams in a week. The platform's algorithms reward social momentum with algorithmic playlist placement that money can't directly buy.

Quick Answers

How quickly can you launch a campaign?

Once your content is approved, campaigns launch within one business day. Full delivery completes within 24–72 hours.

Do I need a full music video, or can I use a simple lyric video?

Both work. Simple lyric videos, visualizers, and even static image clips with audio can perform well. Our team will advise on creative best practices for your specific campaign.

Can I run multiple campaigns for the same track?

Yes. Some clients run staggered campaigns — a launch campaign and a follow-up push 2–3 weeks later. This can extend the chart lifespan of a track significantly.
